Install Order Checklist

Before you start: Disconnect the battery negative terminal. Keep it disconnected until you've finished wiring and verified everything with a multimeter.
  1. Disconnect battery negative. Loosen the 10mm nut on the (−) terminal, lift the cable clear, tuck it aside so it can't spring back onto the post.
  2. Mount the relay somewhere in the engine bay — zip-tie it inside the Joisyoya enclosure or to a wire loom nearby. Coil-up orientation doesn't matter.
  3. Run red 12 AWG from battery (+) → inline 15A fuse holder (within 6" of battery) → relay pin 30. Use loom or split-loom tubing to protect the run.
  4. Run orange 12 AWG from relay pin 87 → enclosure + bus bar. Ring terminal on the bus bar end, female spade on the relay end.
  5. Run black 22 AWG from relay pin 85 → nearest clean chassis ground bolt (sand any paint off, use a star washer).
  6. Run green 22 AWG from relay pin 86 → through firewall grommet → add-a-fuse in passenger fuse box. Use 3A or 5A fuse in the tap slot.
  7. Run black 12 AWG from battery (−) → enclosure − bus bar (or bolt the bus bar directly to chassis near the enclosure).
  8. Multimeter check (battery still disconnected): Verify continuity from pin 85 to chassis ground. Verify no continuity between pin 30 and pin 87 (relay should be open at rest).
  9. Reconnect battery negative.
  10. Trigger check (ignition off): Black probe to chassis ground, red probe to pin 86. Should read 0V.
  11. Trigger check (ignition on, engine off): Same probes. Should read ~12V. Should hear a faint click from the relay as the coil energizes.
  12. Output check (ignition on): Probe + bus bar in the enclosure. Should read ~12V.
  13. Ignition off: + bus bar should drop to 0V. Confirms the relay is doing its job and the enclosure won't drain the battery.

If the green wire trigger circuit reads 0V with ignition on, you're tapped into a non-switched circuit. Try a different fuse position. The cigarette lighter / 12V accessory outlet is the most universally reliable switched source on the 370Z.
